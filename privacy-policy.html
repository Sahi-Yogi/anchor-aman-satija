01 / Information You Share Voluntarily

Booking Enquiries & Direct Contact

When you fill out the Booking Enquiry Form, submit feedback on Testimonials, or reach out via WhatsApp, phone, or email, we receive only the information you choose to share:

  • Contact details such as your full name, phone/WhatsApp number, email address, and family or organization name.
  • Event details including celebration type, estimated crowd size, city/venue, function dates, and script/program notes.
  • Voluntary client testimonials or event feedback submitted for review on our website.
02 / Purpose & Strict Non-Sale Commitment

How Your Information Is Used

Your contact and event details are used strictly to communicate with you about Anchor Aman Satija’s availability, prepare tailored proposals, script custom family/corporate segments, and coordinate logistics with your designated planner or agency.

Zero Broker / Third-Party Resale We never sell, rent, or distribute your mobile number, family wedding details, or corporate guest information to third-party vendor aggregators or marketing brokers.
03 / Third-Party Platforms & Technical Assets

WhatsApp Links, YouTube Showreels & Assets

Our static portfolio website does not deploy intrusive advertising trackers. However, when you actively click WhatsApp booking buttons (wa.me), play embedded showreels in the YouTube video modal, or open external Instagram/YouTube links, those interactions are governed by Meta/WhatsApp and Google/YouTube’s own privacy policies. Fonts and icon stylesheets loaded via Google Fonts and Cloudflare CDN only process standard browser HTTP requests required to display typography and icons reliably.

04 / Stage Media & Client Reviews

Family Privacy & Takedown Support

Stage photographs, highlight clips, and verified client testimonials displayed across Gallery and Testimonials showcase genuine performances. If a private family or corporate host prefers that a specific event image, video reel, or review credit be updated or removed, please contact us directly and we will honor the request promptly.

05 / Access, Updates & Contact

Your Privacy Rights & Data Requests

You may contact Aman Satija Management at any time to verify, update, or request deletion of inquiry messages or details previously shared with us.
