The Stage Phenomenon

Elegance, Energy & Excellence.

Starting his journey in 2014 from Alwar, Rajasthan, Aman Satija (Anchor Aman Preet Satija)—often celebrated by audiences as the "Tiger of Alwar"—has evolved into one of North India’s most requested event anchors and entertainers. Over a span of 10+ years, he has commanded more than 2500+ live stages across Rajasthan, Delhi-NCR, and Pan-India.

His philosophy: "Every celebration is a royal story, and the anchor is the heartbeat of that story." Whether conducting interactive couple & family games at destination sangeet ceremonies, hosting star-studded concerts, or presiding over prestigious corporate galas, Aman blends impeccable wit, respect, and high-octane stage command.

Versatile and multilingual, he bridges diverse guest groups with effortless humor, ensuring nobody feels like a bystander and every moment becomes a lifetime memory.
