Full Official Anchor Profile

Aman Preet Satija ("The Tiger of Alwar") is an acclaimed Indian event anchor, corporate emcee, and destination wedding host performing since 2012. Having commanded over 2,500+ live stages across Rajasthan, Delhi-NCR, Pan-India, and Dubai, he specializes in high-energy destination sangeets, bespoke couple compatibility games, blue-chip corporate summits (Maruti Suzuki, Tata Motors, Samsung, HDFC, ICICI), and arena celebrity concerts—delivered fluently across Hindi, English, Punjabi, and Marwari.

Production Specification Sheet

Technical & Stage Rider

01 / Preferred Cordless Microphone

Sennheiser EW-D / G4 Series or Shure SM58 / Beta 58A Wireless Handheld (strictly fresh lithium/alkaline batteries for every function + 1 synced backup cordless mic ready on stand).

02 / Stage Line-of-Sight & Monitors

2 crisp floor stage monitor wedges (vocal clear) + unobstructed line-of-sight from main stage to DJ console, sound FOH, and visual/LED director.

03 / Pre-Show Soundcheck Window

30 to 45 minutes dedicated mic gain calibration, EQ check, and background sting/walk-on track check prior to guest entry.

04 / Corporate AV & Teleprompter

For conferences, award ceremonies & AGMs: stage countdown clock and optional confidence monitor / teleprompter placed cleanly downstage.

05 / Hospitality & Greenroom

Clean, private lockable waiting room/greenroom near the venue with warm water, mirror, and charging access prior to showtime.
