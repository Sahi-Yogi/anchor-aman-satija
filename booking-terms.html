Every celebration deserves effortless coordination. Below are the standard engagement terms governing all direct and agency bookings with Anchor Aman Satija.

01 / Date Blocking

Enquiries vs. Confirmed Block

Submitting the online enquiry form, calling, or checking quotes over WhatsApp checks tentative availability only. Your event dates are formally locked on Aman’s calendar strictly upon written confirmation and receipt of the agreed booking retainer.

02 / Commercial Terms

Retainer & Milestone Settlement

Bookings follow a standard two-part schedule: an initial booking advance/retainer to reserve the dates exclusively, followed by balance settlement prior to event travel or show commencement as specified in your written booking confirmation.

03 / Outstation Protocol

Travel, Stay & Local Transfers

For events outside Jaipur & Alwar, return travel (air/rail as agreed), safe local transfers, and a clean private hotel room near the event venue are arranged by the host or included in a turnkey package quoted upfront before confirmation.

04 / Stage & Soundcheck

Technical Rider & Greenroom

Hosts/production teams ensure working cordless microphones (Sennheiser/Shure quality), backup mics, stage monitors, and a 30–45 minute pre-show soundcheck window. Full specifications are available on the Tech Rider Sheet.

05 / Cancellations

Date Shifts & Rescheduling

Booking retainers compensate for turning away competing enquiries on locked dates and are non-refundable in case of host cancellation. When notified early, retainers may be adjusted once toward rescheduled dates within 6 months, subject to calendar availability.

06 / Custom Mandates

Function Hours & Scope

Each function block (Haldi, Mehndi, Sangeet, Wedding, Corporate Gala) specifies agreed show timings and deliverables upfront. Extended stage hours, extra days added late, or international visa requirements are quoted transparently prior to execution.

Important Precedence Notice

2. Artist Safety & Stage Etiquette: Hosts and event management agencies are responsible for ensuring a respectful, secure working atmosphere backstage and on stage, free from physical harassment or unsafe stage electrical/pyro rigging.
